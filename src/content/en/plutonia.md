Plutonia is a contemporary art and jewelry brand that transforms everyday objects, food-related memories and references to popular culture into original art pieces. The challenge was to translate this identity into a digital experience that went beyond a conventional online store, letting visitors discover the brand's conceptual universe before exploring its products.

The solution combined visual narrative and custom Front-End development. I designed and implemented a *scroll storytelling* experience using GSAP and ScrollTrigger, integrating visual compositions, transitions and scroll-driven animations. The site was built with WordPress and WooCommerce, incorporating theme customizations, custom styles and a responsive interface connecting the editorial experience with the e-commerce functionality.

The result is a platform that brings together brand narrative, interactive design and e-commerce in a coherent digital experience. Visitors can explore Plutonia's creative universe, discover its collections and access both its products and its workshops. The project reflects my ability to combine art direction, Front-End development, JavaScript animation and WordPress and WooCommerce customization, seeking a balance between visual experimentation, usability and performance.

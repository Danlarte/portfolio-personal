I carried out a technical audit that identified problems with the installation and licensing of Elementor Pro, as well as compatibility conflicts that affected site editing. I coordinated the regularization of the official license and worked on a clean WordPress installation, updating the PHP environment, reorganizing themes and reducing installed plugins from 27 to 10.

I also optimized images, scripts and cache rules; configured Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager with key events; and implemented forms protected with reCAPTCHA and WhatsApp messaging. The work included load testing, comparative PageSpeed Insights measurements and recommendations to improve the mobile experience and the site's infrastructure.

In a second intervention, I responded to a security incident that compromised the site. To keep commercial channels operational during the recovery, I developed a temporary landing page with direct access to the external booking system and WhatsApp contact. I then carried out scanning, cleaning and WordPress recovery tasks, prioritizing service continuity and the security of the installation.

YOKO Beauty is a Korean cosmetics brand that aims to bring the skincare universe closer through a contemporary, attractive and accessible identity. The challenge was to build a brand from its foundations, defining a visual language of its own and translating it into a digital shopping experience able to communicate the characteristics, benefits and particularities of its products in a clear and organized way.

The solution covered the full development of the brand identity, from naming and visual design to art direction and the implementation of the e-commerce. I designed and built the online store using WordPress and WooCommerce, working on the catalog structure, product presentation and browsing experience. Each product page was organized to provide detailed information on ingredients, benefits, skin types, modes of use and recommendations, making the catalog easy to explore and the products easy to understand.

The result is an e-commerce platform that integrates visual identity, specialized content and functionality into a coherent digital experience. YOKO Beauty has a brand system applied to its online presence and a store designed to accompany users as they discover and select products. The project reflects my ability to approach a digital development end to end, combining branding, art direction, interface design and development with WordPress and WooCommerce.

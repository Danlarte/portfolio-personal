RUAJ Vajilla Antigua is a brand dedicated to selling tableware and antique objects, where each piece has its own aesthetic characteristics and identity. The challenge was to translate that universe into an online store that respected the character of the products, offering a carefully crafted visual presentation and a clear browsing experience that made it easy to discover the different pieces available.

The solution was the full design and development of the website using WordPress and WooCommerce. I worked on the page structure, catalog organization, interface design and responsive adaptation, seeking a visual composition that gave prominence to the photography and the details of each object. The implementation combined editorial design criteria, visual hierarchy and usability to build a functional store coherent with RUAJ's identity.

The result is an e-commerce platform that brings RUAJ's catalog together in an organized, visually consistent digital environment. The experience lets users explore products and learn about their features through an interface that prioritizes clarity and the presentation of the pieces. The project reflects my ability to autonomously handle the design and development of an online store, integrating UX/UI criteria, Front-End development and WordPress and WooCommerce customization.
